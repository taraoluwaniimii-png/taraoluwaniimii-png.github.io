About

Hands-on, curious, and always building.

I'm a hands-on AI content creator and digital researcher based in Abuja, Nigeria. I learn by experimenting and building: combining AI writing, research, video, voice, and editing tools into complete production workflows — from a raw idea to published content.


My background is in education, research, and communication, and I bring those strengths with me: the discipline to research deeply, the clarity to explain complex ideas simply, and the consistency to ship on schedule. I'm now deliberately expanding into automation, technical tools, and AI-assisted development — not by collecting certificates, but by building real things in public.
